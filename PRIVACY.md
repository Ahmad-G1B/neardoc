# Privacy Policy — Bridge

Bridge is a **100% local-only** companion application for macOS and Android.

- **No cloud servers or accounts:** Bridge does not require sign-in and never transmits your screen, files, clipboard, notifications, or device identifiers over the internet.
- **Local-only communication:** All traffic stays strictly between your Mac and your Android phone over USB or your local Wi-Fi network, authenticated using a one-time QR pairing handshake and per-session HMAC-SHA256 verification.
- **Opt-in permissions:**
  - **Camera (Android):** Used only when you explicitly scan a Bridge QR pairing code.
  - **Notification Access (Android):** Optional; enabled only if you manually toggle **Bridge Notifications** in Android Settings to forward alerts to your Mac's Notification Center.
  - **Local Network & Notifications (macOS):** Used only to communicate with your paired phone on your local Wi-Fi and display forwarded phone alerts.
- **Zero analytics or telemetry:** Bridge contains no ads, trackers, or third-party analytics SDKs.
